    Remarks

    Steps every rig through one full gait cycle at a fixed phase increment, applies each space in turn, and reads the resulting bone positions — not the requested targets. The difference between the two is the whole point: a space that cannot reach the target reports a smaller step than was authored.

    Phase is set explicitly rather than advanced by time, so noise and jitter are frozen and two runs of the same scene give identical numbers.
