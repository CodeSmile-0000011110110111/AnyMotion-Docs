    Class WalkGaitParameters.AsymmetryGroup

    L19 (limp / asymmetric gait): per-leg divergence for one selected leg, applied in WalkGait.EvaluateLeg. The pelvis and torso response (favouring the good leg, rolling onto it, winding up against it) is not authored here — it already falls out of WalkGait.Evaluate's existing leg-differential and weight-balance terms once the two legs' strides diverge, since those derive from leg state rather than from independent oscillators.
