Remarks

Written as a PropertyDrawer rather than as part of the WalkGait inspector, so the buttons appear wherever the parameters are shown without that inspector needing to know about them.

Defaults come from constructing a fresh instance of the group, so the field initialisers in WalkGaitParameters are the single definition of "default" and cannot drift out of step with a duplicated list here.
