    Class WalkGaitParameters.SlopeGroup

    L08 (slope adaptation): the authored half. Extends L05 (foot planting on uneven ground) — the ground normals it tilts the pelvis towards and the reach deficit it draws step-shortening from both come from L05's existing raycast, so there is no separate authored slope input; only how much of that already-measured slope shows up in the pelvis and the stride is authored here. The raycast, blend and pelvis-rotation math are runtime behaviour in IkTargetSpaceDriver, not authored data, so they are not here — see roadmap.md §1a.

    StepShortenRatio

    Declaration
    [Tooltip("How much of a leg's slope-induced reach deficit is attempted by shortening its stride instead of lowering the pelvis, 0..1. 0 is L05's original behaviour: the whole deficit lowers the pelvis. Above 0, the foot target is first pulled back towards directly under the hip; the pelvis then only drops for whatever that did not resolve, so a deficit shortening the stride cannot actually help with — mostly vertical, not fore/aft, which is the ordinary case standing on a slope — still lowers the pelvis exactly as before. Raise this to reduce a walking gait's pelvis sag on a slope in favour of a shorter stride; it will not by itself remove slack the pelvis genuinely needs.")]
