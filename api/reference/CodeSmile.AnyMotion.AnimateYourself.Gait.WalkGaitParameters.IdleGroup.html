    HeadSettleWeight

    Declaration
    [Tooltip("How fully the head's walk/run-cycle bob and yaw settle when idle. 1 stops it completely, same as every other joint. 0 leaves that motion running at full strength even at a full stop, instead of easing to still — useful for Horror, where a head that never quite stops reads as more unsettling than one that goes fully still.
